Healthcare M&A

Walgreens / Sycamore Take-Private

Independent analysis of a $23.7B take-private — the largest retail pharmacy transaction on record.

↗ Presentation ↓ Excel Model
Problem & Approach

WBA's equity had declined 92% from its 2015 peak — driven by structural pharmacy reimbursement compression, $8.9B in healthcare platform write-downs, and a January 2024 dividend suspension. The transaction structure, particularly a Divested Asset Proceed right tied to VillageMD monetization, created valuation complexity that required independent decomposition.


Analyzed deal mechanics using WBA press releases, 8-K filings, and FY2024 10-K. Built a complete transaction timeline, modeled the consideration structure, and assessed the strategic rationale from both sides of the negotiation.

Findings

The $11.45 cash + up to $3.00 DAP right structure is a precise record of where buyer and seller could not agree on value — making this a landmark case in contingent consideration design.

Post-close five-entity separation (Walgreens, Boots, Shields, CareCentrix, VillageMD) signals PE thesis of discrete asset optionality, not consolidated platform growth.

~8,700 US stores failed to insulate WBA from structural margin pressure — scale is not a moat in retail pharmacy.

AI Workflow Design

Enterprise Workflow Redesign

Mapped, evaluated, and restructured a manual multi-step operational process using AI tooling.

Problem & Approach

A recurring process required 6–8 hours of manual effort per cycle: pulling data from multiple sources, reformatting, applying judgment rules, and producing a standardized output. The process was inconsistent across team members and difficult to audit or hand off.


Mapped the existing workflow step by step, classified each stage as rule-based or judgment-dependent, and identified the highest-friction points. Designed a system that automated the rule-based stages and standardized the judgment-stage inputs and outputs.

Outcomes

Cycle time reduced from 6–8 hours to under 45 minutes per run through AI-assisted data normalization and templated output generation.

Output variability eliminated — a standardized format replaced ad-hoc documents, enabling consistent review and easier auditing.

Process became fully trainable — new team members could produce correct output from day one, eliminating informal knowledge dependency.

Systems Design

Retirement Income Planning Tool

Replaced manual salary-to-take-home lookups with a self-contained interactive tool covering all 50 states, local taxes, and cost-of-living adjustments.

↗ Live Tool
Problem & Approach

Students needed to model real post-tax income across different states, cities, and filing statuses — but no tool combined federal brackets, state brackets, local taxes, and cost-of-living into a single calculation. Manual spreadsheet lookups were error-prone and couldn't handle the variable combinations required.


Built a single-file web application encoding all 50 state tax schedules, local tax rates for major cities, filing status variations, Social Security and Medicare, and cost-of-living multipliers into a unified calculation engine. No external API or database required.

Outcomes

Deployed as a live working tool — replaced manual lookups for a full course section, giving students accurate federal + state + city tax modeling with COL-adjusted purchasing power.

Handles edge cases impossible to replicate manually — city-level income taxes (NYC, Philadelphia, Detroit), tiered deduction stacking, and spouse/dependent modeling.

Zero ongoing maintenance — self-contained, no dependencies, fully functional offline.

Analytical Frameworks

AI Workflow Evaluation

How I assess whether a process is a candidate for AI-driven redesign — and what to actually build.

01
Map the current state
Document every step, decision point, and handoff. Most processes have more steps than anyone can articulate from memory.
02
Classify each step
Rule-based vs. judgment-dependent. Automation is straightforward for rules; judgment stages need a different design.
03
Identify the friction points
Where does time accumulate? Where does quality degrade? These are the highest-value targets.
04
Design the system
Match tooling to the classification. Build for standardized output, not just speed.
05
Measure and document
Before/after metrics. What changed, by how much, and why the improvement is durable.

M&A Transaction Decomposition

How I approach analyzing a transaction — particularly structures involving contingent consideration or complex post-close designs.

01
Establish facts from primary sources
Press releases, 8-Ks, SEC filings. Not summaries or press coverage.
02
Map the consideration structure
Cash, contingent, assumed liabilities. Where is value uncertain, and why?
03
Explain why the deal happened now
Equity trajectory, strategic exhaustion, PE timing — what made this the right moment?
04
Read the structure as a negotiation record
Deal structure reveals where the two sides agreed and where they couldn't. Interpret it as data, not just terms.
05
Frame the open questions
What has to be true for the thesis to work? What remains uncertain, and what would resolve it?
